Inside the editor

Your career.
A clearer view.

Open your saved .fm career and work with the players in that save. From a quick squad check to a carefully filtered prospect search, everything starts in one workspace.

01 / SCOUT

Find the right players.

Search all players, your club, another club, a competition, a shortlist or unregistered players. Combine age, positions, reputation, abilities, attributes and available contract dates. Press Enter to run a search. Filters always stay inside your chosen scope.

Save searches you want to use again.
02 / ARRANGE

A table that works your way.

Choose, hide and reorder columns from the same 83 fields you can filter. Sort positions from goalkeeper to forwards, compare two to four players with grouped attributes and verified details, and export results to CSV or JSON.

The information you need, where you need it.
03 / EDIT

See potential. Make changes.

View and edit Current Ability and Potential Ability on the 1–200 scale, plus visible attributes on the familiar 1–20 scale and home/current/world reputation from 0–10,000. Keep changes pending, undo them and review exact before/after bulk values before staging.

Reusable attribute templates are included.
04 / SHORTLIST

Your lists. Back in the game.

Load existing in-game shortlists, add or remove players, create lists and rename or delete custom lists. Save an edited career copy, then load it in FM to use those changes.

Real career lists, with the default list preserved.
05 / DETAILS

More of the picture.

Review player identity, registration, physical details, corroborated contract dates and saved rolling medical records. Club pages show verified main-stadium measurements where available.

Contract, medical and stadium details are read only. Medical records do not establish current fitness.
06 / EXPLORE

Follow the connections.

Open known clubs, nations and competitions from tables and profiles. Back restores your scope, filters, selection and profile, with pending edits preserved. Clearer tabs and restrained colour keep your place visible.

Known relationships link directly; unavailable information stays unknown.

Every edit starts with a copy.

Save Edited Copy creates and verifies a new career file. Your original is never overwritten, and the running game changes only when you load the edited copy.

From save to game

Five steps.
One fresh perspective.

  1. Save in Football Manager.

    Finish saving your current career before opening it in the editor.

  2. Open your career.

    Choose Open Career and select the saved .fm file. The game doesn’t need to be running. A skippable tour helps you get started; replay it from Settings.

  3. Scout, shortlist or edit.

    Browse players, refine your search and stage your changes. Undo or discard before saving.

  4. Save an edited copy.

    Give the new career a different filename. The editor verifies the output.

  5. Load that copy in FM.

    Use Load Game → Local. After playing and saving again, reopen your latest save in the editor.

A few useful answers

Before you
open a save.

Is this a free FM27 editor for Mac?

Yes. emr27 save editor is free to use, modify and distribute under the MIT licence. It is built for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 13 or later. Intel Macs, Windows and Linux are not supported by this release.

Does it edit the running game?

It edits saved career files. Save in Football Manager, open that save in the app, then use Save Edited Copy. Changes appear in FM after you load the new copy. No Windows, CrossOver, separate Python installation or running game is required.

Can I see and edit current and potential ability?

Yes. Current Ability and Potential Ability are viewable and editable from 1 to 200; visible attributes are editable from 1 to 20. FM may rebalance attributes to match current ability. Potential ability is a development ceiling, not a guarantee of progress.

Do the shortlists work in Football Manager?

Yes, for the real lists embedded in supported single-manager careers. Edit list membership or create a new list, save an edited career copy and load it in FM. Standalone .fmf shortlist import and export are not supported. FM’s recruitment views may hide your own club’s players even when they belong to a list.

Will it work after an FM27 update?

Only build 27.0.0+2486695 is currently supported. Other builds are rejected rather than edited with an unverified format. Check these release notes before using a newer game version.

What can’t this version edit?

Contract dates, saved medical records and main-stadium details are read only. The medical view covers rolling records, not complete career history or current fitness. The release does not edit contracts, transfers, club finances, staff, injuries, bans or promises. Positions and feet are view only. Asking price, media descriptions and unverified hidden or personality fields are not exposed. Some registration links remain unresolved; competition scopes appear only where membership can be read.

Does my career leave my Mac?

Career processing happens locally in the Mac app; no save upload is required. This webpage is for information and downloads, and does not accept career files. Downloading the app uses Google Drive.

How have edited saves been checked?

Disposable edited careers have been loaded in FM to confirm an attribute edit, reputation edits including zero and 10,000, and shortlist creation, membership changes, renaming and deletion. Automated checks also verify read-back and archive preservation. Long-term development, list expiry and re-saving behaviour have not been separately verified. Keep your original career as a backup.
